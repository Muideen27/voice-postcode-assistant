# Your new postcode

Nigeria's new postcodes have been live since 1 October 2026.

A postcode has five parts and is written like this: AA-99-H77-BB-55.

- AA is the state.
- 99 is the Local Government Area (LGA).
- H77 is the district.
- BB is the area.
- 55 is the unit.

Example: LA-11-A12-GN-01. Here LA is the state, 11 is the Local Government Area, A12 is the district, GN is the area and 01 is the unit.

How to get your postcode here: share your location, and we look up the postcode for where you are.

How to check a postcode here: say it or type it, and we tell you if it is valid.

We cannot give your postcode from a place name alone. Please share your location instead.
