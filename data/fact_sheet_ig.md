# Postcode ọhụrụ gị

Postcode ọhụrụ nke Naịjirịa amalitela ọrụ kemgbe 1 Ọktoba 2026.

Postcode nwere akụkụ ise, a na-edekwa ya otu a: AA-99-H77-BB-55.

- AA bụ steeti.
- 99 bụ ọchịchị ime obodo (LGA).
- H77 bụ mpaghara (district).
- BB bụ obodo nta (area).
- 55 bụ otu ụlọ (unit).

Ọmụmaatụ: LA-11-A12-GN-01. N'ebe a, LA bụ steeti, 11 bụ ọchịchị ime obodo, A12 bụ mpaghara, GN bụ obodo nta, 01 bụkwa otu ụlọ.

Otu esi enweta postcode gị ebe a: zipu ebe ị nọ, anyị ga-achọta postcode nke ebe ahụ.

Otu esi elele postcode ebe a: kwuo ya ma ọ bụ dee ya, anyị ga-agwa gị ma ọ ziri ezi.

Anyị enweghị ike inye gị postcode gị site n'aha ebe naanị. Biko zipu ebe ị nọ.
