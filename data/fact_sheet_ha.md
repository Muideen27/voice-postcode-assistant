# Sabuwar postcode ɗinka

Sabbin postcode na Najeriya sun fara aiki tun daga 1 ga Oktoba 2026.

Postcode tana da sassa biyar, kuma ana rubuta ta kamar haka: AA-99-H77-BB-55.

- AA ita ce jiha.
- 99 ita ce karamar hukuma (LGA).
- H77 ita ce gunduma (district).
- BB ita ce unguwa (area).
- 55 shi ne rukunin gini (unit).

Misali: LA-11-A12-GN-01. A nan, LA ita ce jiha, 11 karamar hukuma, A12 gunduma, GN unguwa, 01 kuma rukunin gini.

Yadda za ka sami postcode ɗinka a nan: aika wurinka, mu nemo postcode na inda kake.

Yadda za ka duba postcode a nan: faɗe ta ko ka rubuta ta, mu gaya maka ko ingantacciya ce.

Ba za mu iya ba ka postcode ɗinka daga sunan wuri kawai ba. Don Allah ka aika wurinka.
